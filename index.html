5 Golden Rules

  1. Legs + Jump on Monday.
  2. No two consecutive hard basketball games. Alternate hard game and skill day.
  3. Jump and sprint while fresh.
  4. Avoid failure in the gym.
  5. Strict sleep window: 11:30 PM – 7:30 AM.
